## Business Objective
Identify the key drivers of customer retention and translate findings into actionable marketing strategies.

## Methodology
Applied linear regression modeling to estimate the marginal effect of behavioral and perceptual factors on retention likelihood.

## Key Drivers
- Reason 3: +2.94% retention likelihood  
- Reason 8: −5.75% retention likelihood  
- Negative WOM: −41.14% retention  
- Positive WOM: +8.22% retention  

## Strategic Recommendations
- Target high-retention segments with testimonial-driven marketing  
- Redesign messaging for low-retention segments  
- Leverage positive WOM through online advocacy and UGC campaigns
